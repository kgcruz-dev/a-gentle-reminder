A letter for you

Darling,

I know lately it hasn’t been easy. I know that sometimes the things people say or do can make you question yourself, even when those words don’t tell the whole story of who you are.

But I hope you remember this: other people’s opinions do not define your worth. They only show you how those people see things, not everything that makes you, you.

And about feeling unlucky — having a hard time doesn’t mean you are bad luck. It doesn’t mean you bring misfortune to the people around you. You are not a burden, and you do not have to apologize for things you cannot control.

You don't have to be positive every day. You are allowed to feel sad, to rest, to have doubts, and to begin again. None of that makes you weak.

I hope you slowly learn to see yourself with the same gentleness you give the people you care about. You deserve kindness, especially from yourself.

And just so you know: your presence in my life isn't something I blame for the difficult days. I care about you because you're you.

One day at a time, okay? You don't need to figure everything out today.

With care, always ♡
